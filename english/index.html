🌏 The Tradition Behind This BaZi Calculator

This BaZi calculator was built by someone from Putian (莆田), Fujian, China — the birthplace of Mazu (妈祖), the sea goddess worshipped by 300 million people across 50 countries. Mazu was a real person, Lin Mo (林默), born in 960 AD on Meizhou Island. Recognizing her spiritual purity and divine connection, the heavens chose her to protect seafarers and guide lost ships home. Over 1,000 years, 22 emperors granted her 36 imperial titles — from "Lady" to "Heavenly Empress." In 2009, UNESCO recognized Mazu belief and customs as Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

The region's spiritual tradition traces even further back to the ancient Wu-Nuo (巫傩) culture of the Shang and Zhou dynasties (3,000+ years ago), which blended ancestor veneration, nature worship, and ritual ceremony. Nuo mask dances are still performed in Fujian villages during festivals today. This is the authentic cultural root from which Chinese folk divination — including BaZi — grew. 📚 BaZi Knowledge Base

What is BaZi (八字)?
BaZi, the Four Pillars of Destiny, is an ancient Chinese metaphysical system that maps the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) across your birth year, month, day, and hour. Unlike Western sun-sign astrology, BaZi creates a unique destiny code based on your EXACT birth time — the same birth data always produces the same chart, making it a mathematical system closer to astronomy than mysticism. Chinese emperors and scholars have used it for over 2,000 years.
How Does BaZi Work?
Your birth time is converted into Four Pillars using the traditional Chinese calendar (solar longitude / Jieqi). Each pillar has a Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch, mapping to the Five Elements. This reveals your Day Master (core personality), Favorable Element (what balances you), Ten Gods (relationship patterns), Spirit Stars (hidden talents), and Major Luck Cycles (life chapters).
BaZi vs Western Astrology
Western astrology focuses on planetary positions at birth. BaZi analyzes the Five Elements' generating (生) and controlling (克) cycles in your chart. BaZi provides specific guidance for career, wealth, relationships, and life timing through Major Luck Cycles (大运) — 10-year periods that show when different energies activate in your life.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is BaZi fortune telling real?
BaZi is not fortune-telling by intuition or psychic ability. It is a mathematical system — the same birth data always produces the same chart and analysis. Think of it as a map of your elemental energies rather than a prediction. It reveals tendencies, optimal timing, and areas of natural strength, but your choices ultimately shape your path.
Q: What is a Day Master in BaZi?
Your Day Master is the Heavenly Stem of your Day Pillar — it represents your core self, personality, and essential nature. There are 10 Day Master types (Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, Gui), each with distinct characteristics. Your Day Master element determines how the other elements in your chart interact with you.
Q: What is a Favorable Element (用神)?
Your Favorable Element is the Five Elements energy that balances your chart. If your chart has too much Fire, Water may be your Favorable Element. Surrounding yourself with this element — through colors, career choices, directions, and lifestyle — helps harmonize your energy and improve fortune.

Q: What are Major Luck Cycles (大运)?
